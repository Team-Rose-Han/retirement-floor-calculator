Your Safe Retirement Floor

This tool finds the minimum that your Money Flywheel needs to invest for retirement every month so that Future You will be covered no matter what.

  • Above the floor → everything extra is eligible to put into the Spending, Upcoming, or Dream Fund guilt-free, hence “floor-then-dreams”
  • Below the floor → hitting this becomes your next Financial Goal after getting 1 month ahead on bills, paying off credit card debt, maxing out 401(k) match, and saving up a 6-month emergency fund
